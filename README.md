# 🧠 International Community for AI in MS (iAIMS) Wiki

Welcome to the central knowledge repository for the **International Community for Artificial Intelligence in Multiple Sclerosis**. 

This wiki brings together researchers, clinicians, data scientists, software engineers, and patient advocates worldwide to accelerate AI-driven advancements in MS research, diagnosis, treatment, and care.

---

## 📚 What to Expect

This wiki serves as an open-access, community-maintained knowledge hub. Here is what you will find inside:

* **Guides & Tutorials:** Step-by-step walkthroughs
* **Domain Glossaries:** Definitions on MS- and AI-related topics
* **Datasets & Benchmarks:** Index of open-access MS datasets, tools, etc
* **State of the Art (SOTA) Summaries:** High-level summaries of landmark papers at the intersection of AI and MS.
* **Best Practices & Ethics:** Guidelines for data privacy, algorithmic bias reduction, and safe clinical translation

---

## 🤝 How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from everyone—whether you are writing code, explaining a clinical concept, or fixing typos!

### 1. Contribution Workflow
1. **Explore Existing Content:** Check existing pages to see where your expertise fits best.
2. **Open an Issue / Pitch an Update:** For major additions or structural updates, open a GitHub Issue first to discuss your idea with community maintainers.
3. **Fork & Branch:** Create a feature branch off of `main` (e.g., `feature/add-mri-segmentation-tutorial`).
4. **Submit a Pull Request (PR):** Fill out our PR template detailing your changes and link any relevant issues.

### 2. Ways You Can Help
* ✍️ **Write Content:** Draft tutorials, summarize recent literature, or expand clinical/technical glossary terms.
* 🔍 **Review Content:** Peer-review pending PRs for clinical accuracy or technical correctness.
* 🌐 **Localization & Accessibility:** Help translate pages or improve accessibility for non-native readers.
* 🛠️ **Maintain Infrastructure:** Improve search indexing, fix broken links, or refine wiki navigation.

---

## 📋 Content Guidelines

To maintain quality and clarity across diverse disciplines:
* **Target Clear Communication:** Remember our audience spans both clinical and technical backgrounds. Define complex terms or link to the internal Glossary.
* **Cite Sources:** Always cite peer-reviewed research, public datasets, or official guidelines when introducing scientific facts.
* **Maintain Privacy:** **Never** upload, commit, or link to patient data or proprietary/restricted clinical records.
