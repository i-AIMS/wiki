# Tools and Principles
One of the core values of iAIMS is open science research. Especially as AI is at risk of fuelling a new reproducibility crisis like we have seen in psychology (cfr. this [Nature article](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-03817-6)), it is important to ensure reproducibility of our experiments.
Just some examples of why AI research might not be reproducible:

- Different test sets are used by different studies
- Retraining a model with all the same configurations and resources probably results in a different model as many optimisation functions rely on a degree of randomness (e.g. [Stochastic Gradient Descent](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/machine-learning/ml-stochastic-gradient-descent-sgd/))
- Data sets are often not open source, or not even with conditional access.

To maximise reproducibility, Open Science is a great leap forward! In this Wiki Page, we offer practical guidance.
